Payroll

Monthly salary divided by the calendar days in the month, multiplied by the days paid. Every day is paid unless it is leave or unexplained absence, so Sundays and holidays are paid outright and lateness costs nothing. The count runs to the last date you uploaded a file for, so mid-month this is a real accrual, not the full month's salary.
